Playing / 玩法

Slide containers along their length into matching-color exits. Clear the board to win. Standard levels work offline. Hints are free; undo and removal use inventory purchased with earned game coins. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

沿货柜长边拖动，从同色出口送出，清空棋盘即可过关。标准关卡可离线游玩，提示免费；撤销和移除消耗用游戏金币兑换的道具。本版本没有现金内购或订阅。

Progress / 进度

Progress is stored on this device. There is no in-game account or cross-device sync. Reset Progress clears local game progress. Uninstalling can remove progress; OS backups are managed separately.

进度保存在本机，无游戏账号或跨设备同步。清除进度会重置本机游戏存档；卸载可能移除进度，系统备份单独管理。

Ads / 广告

Rewarded ads need a network connection and available inventory. A reward is granted only after the SDK confirms it was earned. Settings > Privacy Policy > Privacy choices manages applicable consent forms.

激励广告需要网络和可用广告库存，仅在 SDK 确认完成后发奖。可在设置的隐私政策中管理适用的隐私选择。
