Privacy Policy

About this policy

This policy explains how Maze Toy Adventure, a Toyloom Games puzzle game, handles information and how these privacy pages work. The game is currently in development. This policy covers the game features described here; any material changes will be reflected in an updated policy. Privacy contact: seansheaton@gmail.com.

Offline play and local progress

The core game works offline and does not require an account. It has no advertising, in-app purchases, integrated analytics, advertising SDKs or developer-operated cloud saves. The game does not transmit your gameplay records to us. It stores progress, move history, best move counts, stars, language, sound/motion preferences and tutorial/review-invitation status on your device, so you can continue playing and retain your choices. These records are not used for advertising or profiling.

Device and platform services

Optional vibration uses the device vibration feature. The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location. Your device, operating system, app store or backup provider may separately process installation, security, diagnostics or backup information under their own settings and policies. Depending on those settings and the installed version, system backup or device transfer may copy or restore local app data. We do not operate or access those backups. Stores may make crash or compatibility reports available to developers; we may use those platform reports to resolve problems without adding an in-game telemetry service.

Purchases and optional ratings

The planned Google Play release is a paid download with all 50 puzzles included. Google Play handles purchase, payment and refund processing. The game does not request or receive your full payment-card details. Google may provide sales or transaction records to the developer for purchase support, accounting and legal obligations. Optional ratings open the store after a parent check; rating is not required to continue playing. Store activity is governed by the store provider’s policy, including Google’s Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Parent feedback and support

A grown-up can choose Contact & feedback to open an email draft addressed to seansheaton@gmail.com. The game does not send anything automatically or attach screenshots, identifiers or save files. If you send the email, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to include through our email provider. We use them to answer your request, investigate the issue and handle privacy enquiries, not for advertising or mailing lists. Please do not include a child’s name, photo or other identifying information, passwords or payment-card details. External email and store actions may need internet access.

Children and family play

The game is designed for children and family play. It has no chat, public uploads or forms asking children for personal information. External feedback and store links use a parent check; this is a product safeguard, not identity verification or verified parental consent. If you believe a child has sent personal information that should not be retained, contact us so we can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion. Purchases and device accounts remain subject to platform parental controls.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends ordinary request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to the hosting provider. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies or a contact form to these policy pages. GitHub handles hosting information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Retention, sharing and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear or remove it. Settings → Grown-ups → Erase local progress clears game progress, preferences and the game’s recovery copy. Device settings may also allow you to clear app storage or uninstall the app. These actions do not necessarily delete system backups, sent emails or store purchase records; manage those with the relevant provider. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up and applicable legal obligations. Purchase records, where received, may be kept for accounting or legal requirements. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Service providers process information as described above; disclosure may also be required by law or to protect legal rights. Providers may process information outside your country under their own policies.

Your choices and contact

You can play without creating an account or contacting us. There is no in-game account to delete. Where applicable law grants rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of personal information we hold, contact seansheaton@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify and handle the request. We cannot remotely access or delete a save held only on your device. Suggested email subject: Maze Toy Adventure Privacy. We will update this page and its effective date if these practices change, and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.
